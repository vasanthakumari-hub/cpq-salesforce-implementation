# Automated Contracting and Renewal Lifecycle — Business Analyst

User Story: As a Sales Ops Admin, I want to automate the creation of Contracts and Subscriptions when an Opportunity is Won so that renewal tracking is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria: 
1. Opportunities must have 'Contracted' checkbox checked to trigger CPQ contracting logic.
2. Subscriptions and Assets must be created from Quote Lines with specific subscription terms.
3. Renewal Forecast and Renewal Quoted fields must automate the creation of renewal opportunities.
